⚡ Core Business Rules Enforced in Service Layer

  • Rule 1 Available Group Pool: Total Contributions minus Outstanding Loans (Loan Amount - Total Repayments).
  • Rule 2 Single Active Loan Policy: A member with an active unpaid loan cannot obtain a second loan.
  • Rule 3 Pool Balance Limit: Loan disbursement cannot exceed the group's current available pool balance.
  • Rule 4 Repayment & Status Transition: Repayment cannot exceed outstanding loan amount; once fully repaid, status updates from ACTIVE to PAID.
